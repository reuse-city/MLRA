# Material Literacy and Resource Assessment (MLRA)

> Co-design of an open learning course and community knowledge infrastructure for inclusive circularity.

A project by **[Global Innovation Gathering (GIG)](https://globalinnovationgathering.org/)** and **[Reuse City](https://github.com/reuse-city)**, supported by the **[Hans Sauer Stiftung](https://www.hanssauerstiftung.de/)** under the funding programme *"Citizen Science… und dann? 2026–2027"*.

---

## 🧭 About the Project

Open workshops, repair cafés, and makerspaces possess deep experiential knowledge about materials, repair, and circular resource management. Building directly upon the findings and open curriculum developed in the citizen science project **[CMVC (Circular Material Valuer Curriculum)](https://github.com/reuse-city/CMVC)**, **MLRA** is translating this practical expertise into an open, low-barrier online course.

The course will be hosted as the flagship module of the **Inclusive Circularity** series on the **Global Innovation Academy (GIA)**—a permanent, community-owned open learning commons.

### Key Objectives
1. **Co-Design with Communities:** Run participatory workshops and interviews with repairers, makers, and educators to refine modules and assess local needs.
2. **Transform & Produce OER:** Convert hands-on repair knowledge into self-paced learning paths (Material Identification, Condition Diagnostics, Value Determination) with visual guides and instructional videos.
3. **Pilot & Validate:** Run a moderated pilot cohort with GIG members and local workshop partners.
4. **Permanent Commons:** Release all curriculum, methodologies, and technical pipelines under open licences (OER) for local adaptation, translation, and long-term re-use.

## 📅 Roadmap (2026–2027)

* **Phase 1: Participatory Setup & Needs Assessment (M1–M2 / Oct–Dec 2026)**  
  Stakeholder outreach, participatory working group sessions, technical LMS exploration.
* **Phase 2: Course Development & OER Production (M3–M5 / Dec 2026–Feb 2027)**  
  Scripting learning pathways, didactic refinement, multimedia asset production, and platform integration.
* **Phase 3: Facilitated Pilot & Community Activation (M6–M7 / Mar–Apr 2027)**  
  Running the moderated course pilot, collecting peer feedback, and drafting local adaptation guidelines.
* **Phase 4: Optimization, Public Launch & Sustainability (M8–M10 / May–Jul 2027)**  
  Public launch of the MOOC on GIA, publishing OER packages, and establishing institutional partnership models.
