🛠️ Calling all makers, repairers, fixers, educators, and circular economy practitioners! 🌍✨

Do you work in a repair café, open workshop, makerspace, or run practical circularity courses? We need your experience!

Building directly on our citizen science findings from the **Circular Material Valuer Curriculum (CMVC)**, GIG is launching an ambitious new initiative supported by the **Hans Sauer Stiftung** (under *"Citizen Science… und dann? 2026–2027"*):

📚 **Material Literacy & Resource Assessment (MLRA)**
We are co-designing a permanent, community-owned open online course (MOOC) on the brand-new **Global Innovation Academy (GIA)** to turn grassroots repair knowledge into open educational resources (OER) that last.
