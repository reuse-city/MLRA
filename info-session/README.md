## 📄 Kickoff Info Session — 29 October 2026

Materials and presentation slides from the initial project information session held on **29 October 2026** with members of the Global Innovation Gathering (GIG) network and partner communities.

* **Presentation Slides (PDF):** [`202610 Info Session HSS.pptx.pdf`](202610%20Info%20Session%20HSS.pptx.pdf)
* **Presenters:** Felipe Schmidt Fonseca & Sandra Mamitzsch (Global Innovation Gathering e.V.)

### Key Topics Presented
1. **How Permanent Are Project Outputs?**
   * Addressing the recurring cycle where valuable community knowledge, curricula, and platforms disappear after project grants expire.
2. **Investing in Platforms That Last:**
   * Combining open-source code, data sovereignty, and community ownership (GIA on Open edX, Appropedia, Codeberg, Zenodo).
3. **Collaboratively Advancing Knowledge of Materials:**
   * Introducing MLRA building on the Citizen Science *Circular Material Valuer Curriculum (CMVC)*.
4. **Next Steps & Call to Action:**
   * Registration open for participatory co-design sessions.
