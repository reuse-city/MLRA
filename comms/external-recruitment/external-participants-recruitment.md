# Call for participants - Material Literacy Education

GIG has a new participatory project and invites people experienced with **repair and material literacy**, as preferably those doing **online education & courses**, to contribute.

The Hans Sauer Foundation - which funded our [Circular Material Valuer](https://globalinnovationgathering.org/circular-material-valuer-curriculum/) project - offered a follow-up opportunity and we took it. It is part of their program [Citizen Science… and then?](https://www.hanssauerstiftung.de/forderprogramm/foerderprogramm-2026-citizen-science-und-dann/). Our proposal? An online course based on the curriculum we co-created last year.  
  
In the coming months, GIG will organise a series of co-design sessions and other collaborative activities to develop contents, explore education platforms, and host a pilot run of the course. If you want to participate, please fill the form available [here](https://cloud.globalinnovationgathering.org/apps/forms/s/3witbBDWjjwPQ5DD6bLosmDj). If you want first to know more about the project, we’re organising an open info session on the 18th of November (calendar link is here).

Agenda: 

- Introducing the new project
- The Global Innovation Academy
- Building on the Circular Material Valuer
- How can you support? 
- Timeline & next steps
